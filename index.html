What is Nekomo

Everything anime, in one app

Born from the ashes of Kuro No and Shiro, Nekomo keeps the spirit of those projects alive as a community-run anime client.

AniList Discovery

Browse, search, and discover anime with rich metadata powered by AniList — seasonal charts, trending titles, and detailed info pages.

Streaming

Watch episodes through community-maintained third-party sources, with a built-in player designed for anime.

Downloads

Save episodes for offline viewing and watch on the go — no connection required.

Watchlist & Tracking

Keep track of what you're watching, plan to watch, and have finished with list syncing and progress tracking.

Community-Run

Maintained by the community, for the community. Suggest features, report bugs, and help shape the project's future on Discord.

Fully Open Source

Every line of code is public on GitHub. Audit it, fork it, or contribute — Nekomo belongs to everyone.
